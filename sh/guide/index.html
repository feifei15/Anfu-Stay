Curtains窗帘指南

There are two ways to open and close the curtains and sheer curtains. 开关窗帘窗纱有两种方式：

Method 1方式一

Remote control遥控

Use the two small remotes mounted on the wall next to the light switch panel. One controls the sheer curtain, the other the blackout curtain. 使用安装在灯光开关面板旁的两个遥控器。一个控制窗纱，一个控制遮光窗帘。

Method 2方式二

Voice control声控

Say to the smart assistant:对小爱同学说：

“小爱同学，把窗帘打开” — open the curtains “小爱同学，把窗帘关了” — close the curtains

A/C空调指南

The same Daikin remote design is used in every room. 每个房间使用相同型号的大金遥控器。

Hot / Cold冷暖切换

Top-left button toggles heating and cooling mode.左上方按钮切换制冷 / 制热模式。

On / Off开关机

The round gold button turns the unit on and off.金色圆形按钮为开关机键。

After pressing On/Off, listen for a beep: two short beeps = On, one long beep = Off. 按下开关后请听提示音：两声短鸣 = 开机，一声长鸣 = 关机。
Pink ribbon = remote-control receptor Silver ribbon = airflow indicator 粉色结 = 遥控信号接收器 银色结 = 出风指示

Dryer烘干机指南

The dryer is the upper machine; the washer is below. 上方是烘干机，下方是洗衣机。

Water-condensate box冷凝水盒

Pull out the box at the upper left and empty all water.拉出烘干机左上方的水盒，并将水全部倒空。

Lint-filter drawer毛屑过滤盒

Open the filter inside the dryer door and remove all lint.打开烘干机门内的过滤盒，并清除所有毛屑。

Before and after every drying cycle: make sure the lint-filter drawer and water-condensate box are empty. 每次烘干前后：请确认毛屑过滤盒已清理，冷凝水盒已倒空。

TV电视指南

Cable TV有线电视

To watch TV, turn it on using the Xiaomi TV remote, switch to HDMI 1, then use the Cable TV remote to operate.观看电视时，请使用小米电视遥控器开机，将输入源切换至 HDMI 1，然后使用有线电视遥控器操作。

Apple TV苹果电视

To stream to Apple TV, unplug the black/yellow HDMI cable from the Cable TV box and connect it to the Apple TV.使用 Apple TV 投屏时，请拔下有线电视盒上的黑黄 HDMI 线，并将其连接到 Apple TV。

06

Bluetooth Speaker蓝牙音箱

To listen to Bluetooth music on the Bose entertainment center in the living room, press Source on the remote until the display shows “A/V (Front)”, then connect your phone’s Bluetooth to “K3 Pro.”

若要使用客厅的 Bose 娱乐系统播放蓝牙音乐，请按遥控器上的 Source 键，直到屏幕显示 “A/V (Front)”，然后在手机蓝牙中连接 “K3 Pro”。

Trash Guide垃圾指南

Follow this route down to the building's trash station. Numbered photos mark each turn along the way. 请按照以下路线前往楼内垃圾站，每张照片标注了沿途的转弯位置。

1

Exit the elevator and head toward the lobby.走出电梯，前往大堂方向。

2

Pass the front desk, following the arrow.经过前台，按照箭头方向前进。

3

Continue down the hallway toward the side door.沿走廊继续前行，走向侧门。

4

Head into the stairwell marked "1F".走进标有 "1F" 的楼梯间。

5

Go down the stairs and continue outside.下楼梯，继续往外走。

6

Sort your trash at the station, dry and wet waste separated.在垃圾站分类投放 — 干湿分离。

垃圾站 · Trash station: please separate dry waste (干垃圾) from wet waste (湿垃圾) as shown on the signage. 垃圾站：请按标识将干垃圾与湿垃圾分开投放。
